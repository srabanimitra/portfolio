CLDT-Net: Cross-Layer Digital Twin for Cascaded IT/OT Attack Detection

+First authorManuscript. Industrial control systems security.

Attackers can break into an IT service and then reach the machines of an industrial plant. Most detectors watch only one side. CLDT-Net watches both and also tells you the attack path.

F1 score
0.967
Clean false alarms
1.4%
Warning before breach
17.8 s
Attack path F1
0.972

F1 against other views (axis from 0 to 1)

  • CLDT-Net0.967
  • IT-only0.952
  • Graph-free0.909
  • OT-only0.854
  • Built DTSim, a six-stage water-treatment IT/OT co-simulation with six attack families mapped to MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, because no public dataset pairs an IT break-in with its physical effect.
  • Model: ontology graph of 63 nodes, relational graph attention, a causal temporal convolutional encoder, a physics-informed forecaster and conformal alert thresholds. 0.42M parameters.
  • Alerts came 17.8 s before the first safety breach on average, close to the 20.2 s an oracle would get.

Stated openly in the paper: results are on simulated data, the ablations do not show which single component drives the gain, and performance drops under heavy sensor noise at a fixed threshold.

Co-authors: Nusrat Jahan Adiba, Sumaiya Aftab, Nabiha Tahsin Khan, Afsana Ahmed Munia, Md Hasan Al Kayem. Department of CSE, AUST.

  • Graph attention

MAPSR-BENCH: Benchmarking Spatial Reasoning in Vision-Language Models over Map-Based Environments

+Submitted to EMNLPVision-language models, benchmarks.

Developed a benchmark comprising 258 map images and 2,457 multiple-choice QA pairs to evaluate spatial reasoning in 13 open-source VLMs. Conducted multi-seed experiments and analyzed model failure patterns across difficulty levels.

Map images
258
Questions
2,457
VLMs tested
13
Reasoning types
5
  • Questions cover directions, relative position, route following, viewpoint-dependent reasoning and mental rotation.
  • Difficulty is rated separately for the map (landmark density, roads, intersections, clutter, orientation) and for the question (direct relation up to multi-step navigation and mental rotation).
  • This shows whether a model fails because the map is hard to read, the spatial step is hard, or both.

  • Benchmark design
  • Spatial reasoning
